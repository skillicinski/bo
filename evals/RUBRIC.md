# Agent summary rubric

- Score each criterion from 1 to 5: 1 = fails the criterion, 2 = major problems, 3 = adequate with noticeable gaps, 4 = strong with minor gaps, and 5 = complete and consistently accurate.
- Factual faithfulness: every claim is supported by its raw document; no invented details. Preserve the source's epistemic framing by distinguishing source-reported facts, the author's experience or measurements, recommendations or opinions, and predictions or forecasts. Do not present the latter three as independently established facts.
- Coverage: every successful raw snapshot has one non-empty summary, including its main subject and useful supporting facts.
- Usefulness: summaries are concise, readable Markdown that preserves the source's important distinctions.
- Page cleanliness: summaries omit navigation, cookie notices, authoring chrome, ads, and unrelated page boilerplate.
