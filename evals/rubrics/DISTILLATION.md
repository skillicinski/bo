# Distill agent rubric

- Score each criterion from 1 to 5: 1 = fails the criterion, 2 = major problems, 3 = adequate with noticeable gaps, 4 = strong with minor gaps, and 5 = complete and consistently accurate.
- Factual faithfulness: every claim is supported by the provenance documents; preserve qualifications, uncertainty, authorship, measurements, recommendations, opinions, and forecasts.
- Cross-source integration: the document identifies useful relationships, agreements, or differences across at least two distinct source identities.
- Usefulness: the document is concise, readable Markdown that helps a reader understand the selected theme.
- Structure: the title, introduction, sections, explanatory paragraphs, and bullets form a clear and complete document.
- Source attribution: section links and the final Sources section identify the documents that support the claims.
